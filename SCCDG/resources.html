Essential Gear Guide

New to the sport? Here is the basic kit recommended for playing your first round!

  • Putter: For short, accurate straight-line throws into the basket.
  • Mid-Range: Highly versatile discs designed to hold reliable flight lines.
  • Fairway Driver: Great for adding distance without sacrificing control on narrow holes.
  • Disc Golf Bag: A lightweight backpack or shoulder pouch to organize your plastic.
